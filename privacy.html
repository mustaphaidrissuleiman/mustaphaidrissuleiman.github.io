Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you interact with this website (mustaphaidris.com), read my essays, or connect with me and my team.

This Privacy Policy provides information on the collection, use, and sharing (collectively referred to as “processing” or “process”) of personal information collected in connection with your use of the website, mobile applications, and social media pages that link to this Privacy Policy, as well as your interactions with me and my team. This Privacy Policy also explains the privacy rights you have in relation to these processing activities.

The Privacy Policy can change over time, for example to comply with legal requirements or to meet changing needs. The most up-to-date version will always be found on this website. In case there is an important change that we want to highlight to you, we will also inform you in another appropriate way (for example, via a notice on the website or direct email update).

As used in this Privacy Policy, ‘personal information’ or ‘personal data’ means information that relates to an identified individual or an identifiable individual. For example, this could include your name, email address, phone number, business contact details, or information gathered through your interactions with us via our website, email, or at agricultural and technology events. Personal information is also referred to as ‘information about you.’ For more detail about the types of information about you that we may process, please refer to Section 2 below.

SECTION 1 – SCOPE OF THE PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy applies to the processing of personal information by:

  • Visitors and readers of the website (mustaphaidris.com) and published essays.
  • Farmers, partners, collaborators, and clients communicating with me or Herdbase Africa.
  • Attendees of workshops, seminars, and speaking sessions where I present on livestock agriculture and AI.
  • Individuals who contact me directly via email, telephone, WhatsApp, or social media channels.

SECTION 2 – CATEGORIES OF YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION WE PROCESS

We process both offline and online personal information. Information about you may in some cases directly identify you, while in other cases it may only indirectly identify you.

The categories of personal information we process include:

  • Contact and Biographical Information: Your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, job title, farm location, or company name when you share them with us.
  • Communications Data: Questions, collaboration proposals, speaking invitations, feedback, and notes from email exchanges or direct conversations.
  • Technical and Device Data: IP address, device type, browser settings, operating system, and date/time of visits when accessing the website.

SECTION 3 – SOURCES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION WE PROCESS

You may provide personal information through direct interaction when you:

  • Visit the website or read my published articles and monographs.
  • Contact me to request information, explore partnerships, or collaborate on livestock tech or AI research.
  • Share your contact card or details with me or my team at conferences, agricultural exhibitions, or virtual seminars.
  • Engage with my content on professional platforms such as LinkedIn, X (Twitter), GitHub, or Hugging Face.

As you interact with our website, we may also automatically collect technical data about your equipment, browsing actions, and patterns using standard server logs and essential session technologies.

SECTION 4 – HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We process your personal information to help us better connect with you. The following are the purposes for which we collect and process your personal information:

  • To provide you with news, research updates, essays, and insights related to Mustapha Idris Suleiman, Herdbase Africa, and applied AI in agriculture.
  • To respond to your inquiries, partnership proposals, advisory requests, and speaking invitations.
  • To monitor, review, evaluate, and improve your reading experience when you visit the website.
  • To analyze traffic on the website, including determining visitor numbers and understanding how readers navigate our essays and monographs.
  • To notify you about important changes or publications on the website.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical, security, or infrastructure issues.
  • To fulfill legal and regulatory obligations under applicable Nigerian data protection laws.

SECTION 5 – SHARING OF YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We respect your privacy and will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. In addition to team members who have a need to know, the following trusted third parties may have access to your information strictly to support our operations:

  • Communication & Email Tools: Trusted platforms that assist in managing direct correspondence and sending out research updates.
  • Affiliated Initiatives: Herdbase Africa and Geeks Arewa team members who require your information for the same collaboration purposes described in this Policy.
  • Professional Infrastructure Providers: Secure web hosting, content delivery, and IT service providers that maintain server uptime and security.

SECTION 6 – YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You have privacy rights regarding the information we process about you. You can choose to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections or updates to any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Request the deletion of your personal information from our active records.
  • Object to or restrict our use of your personal information at any time.
  • Opt out of any direct email updates or publications.

Where you wish to exercise any of your data privacy rights, kindly notify me by sending an email to mustaphaidrissuleiman@gmail.com.

The right to request that we delete your personal information is subject to any retention required by law or necessary for technical security audits.

SECTION 7 – THIRD-PARTY LINKS

This website and our communications may include links to third-party websites, plug-ins, and external platforms (such as LinkedIn, X, GitHub, Hugging Face, or academic portals). Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you.

We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements. When you leave our website, we encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

SECTION 8 – COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to ensure the website operates smoothly and securely. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help remember basic preferences and monitor site stability.

Examples of cookies we use:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies used to operate the website efficiently during your visit.
  • Preference Cookies: Used to remember your view settings and display configurations.
  • Security Cookies: Used for authentication, server integrity, and protection against unauthorized requests.

You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, basic website navigation is fully supported even if you choose to disable optional cookies.

SECURITY & CONFIDENTIALITY

Information submitted by you is stored on secure, encrypted servers, and access is restricted only to authorized persons responsible for maintaining our infrastructure. We have put in place physical, electronic, and procedural processes that safeguard and protect your information against unauthorized access, modification, or erasure.

While no system on the internet is completely foolproof, we maintain rigorous technical standards (including Transport Layer Security / TLS encryption) to safeguard your data. In the unlikely event of a security breach affecting your personal data, such incident will be handled in accordance with established Data Breach Management Procedures and notified to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) within statutory timeframes, with immediate notice provided to affected individuals where appropriate.
