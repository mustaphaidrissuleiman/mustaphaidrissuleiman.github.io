What I’m building

Commercial Venture · Flagship Focus

Herdbase Africa / Herdbase360

An operating and record-keeping platform designed for African livestock businesses. Herdbase360 gives producers structured herd registers, vaccination tracking, and feed expenditure accounting to determine the true cost of production per batch—supported by lightweight WhatsApp logging workflows for frontline farm workers.

Why a Systems Technologist Chose Livestock Agriculture

Working in high-volume banking systems and payment infrastructure taught me an enduring principle: institutions scale when records are auditable, verifiable, and disciplined. Yet across Nigeria and the wider Sahel, livestock—one of the continent's most valuable asset classes—is largely managed without operational data.

I founded Herdbase Africa to apply that same operational discipline to livestock farming. Software cannot guarantee profit, but disciplined record-keeping gives farmers the visibility they need to understand feed conversion, health expenditures, and real margins.

02 —— PAYMENT RAILS
2023
—2025

Payment Infrastructure & Systems Engineering at Unified Payments

At Unified Payments Services PLC, I served as Team Lead for Web Merchant Platforms, overseeing technical integration and operational compliance of EMV 3DS 2.0 transaction authentication platforms.

My team engineered payment gateway connections serving major financial institutions and fintechs—including Remita, Paystack, Flutterwave, and Moniepoint—while deploying the Payloft Gateway to optimize payment routing and merchant settlement reliability.

03 —— BANKING CORE
2022
—2023

Core Banking Infrastructure & Operations at Access Bank

At Access Bank PLC, Nigeria's largest financial institution, I worked in frontline IT operations supporting core banking infrastructure. My mandate was system availability: maintaining transaction audit integrity, monitoring high-throughput database workloads, and diagnosing service issues across nationwide ATM and POS networks.

Managing systems where ledger entries must reconcile accurately every second instilled an uncompromising respect for operational discipline, auditability, and resilient architecture.
