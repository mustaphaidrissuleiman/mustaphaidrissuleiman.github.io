A Letter from the Founder

Why the transformation of Africa's livestock economy cannot wait.

HERDBASE

Africa has always had the people, the land, and the livestock to build a great agricultural economy. What we have not always had are the systems to help that economy reach its full potential.

I know this because I have lived it.

Herdbase began with three friends who were also livestock farmers. We knew the early mornings, the rising cost of feed, the uncertainty of the market, and the daily work that goes into raising healthy animals. We also knew what it meant to run a business without the information and systems needed to truly understand it.

Our records lived in notebooks and in memory. We could not always say which animals were profitable, what it really cost to raise them, or where money was being lost. And if someone had asked us to prove the health and performance of the business, we would have struggled to do so.

We knew we were working hard. What we did not have were the tools to make that hard work count for more.

That experience changed our view of the livestock industry. We began to see that these were not simply problems on our own farms. They were problems shared by livestock businesses across the continent. Farmers were working without reliable records. Businesses were making important decisions without good data. Markets were fragmented. Good livestock businesses often struggled to prove their value or reach the opportunities that could help them grow.

We believed there had to be a better way. That belief became Herdbase Africa.

We have seen what technology can do for other industries across Africa. Finance has changed. Commerce has changed. Logistics has changed. Livestock should not be left behind.

We believe every livestock business deserves the chance to become more productive, more organized, and more competitive, regardless of its size. There is much work ahead. But we know why we started. And we know where we are going.

Mustapha Idris Suleiman Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Herdbase Africa Limited

AI Research & Open Source 2024 – 2026

Afro-XLM-R: Hausa Agricultural NER System

Low-Resource Natural Language Processing for Small-Ruminant Veterinary Health

Master in Information Technology thesis at the University of Lagos, supervised by Prof. V. T. Odumuyiwa. Conducted pioneering research bridging NLP and pastoral husbandry by constructing a 2,000-sentence gold-standard Hausa agricultural corpus (36,082 tokens, 8,568 entity mentions). Fine-tuned Afro-XLM-RoBERTa for token classification across 10 semantic livestock categories.

10-Class Entity Scheme: ANIMAL_TYPE, BREED, QUANTITY, SYMPTOM, DISEASE, MEDICATION, FEED_TYPE, DATE_TIME, LOCATION, PERSONNEL.
Diagnostic Assistant: Hybrid neuro-symbolic chatbot enabling Hausa-speaking farmers to query veterinary protocols and disease remediation.
Hugging Face Weights: Published model weights available at mustaphaidris/hausa-agri-ner-candidate.
