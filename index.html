The Story of a Techno Farmer

Bridging the soil of Nigerian agriculture with cutting-edge artificial intelligence and high-throughput software architecture.

From the Farm Gate to Neural Architectures

I am Mustapha Idris Suleiman, an engineering founder operating at the direct crossroads of African agriculture and modern computing. In the agricultural corridors of Nigeria—from Oyo and Lagos to Kano and Katsina—farming is not a theoretical abstraction; it is the heartbeat of millions of livelihoods.

As the CEO and Founder of Herdbase Africa, I have engineered end-to-end digital systems managing livestock inventory, veterinary treatment cycles, and meat value chains. But software is only as good as its usability for the people who need it most.

That conviction led to my work in Low-Resource African Language NLP. When pastoralists and smallholders need advice on sheep (*tumaki*) or goats (*awaki*), generic English models fall short. I trained and released domain-specific models like hausa-agri-ner-candidate on Afro-XLM-RoBERTa, enabling natural language diagnostic interfaces in Hausa that understand agricultural nuance and veterinary symptoms.

Machine Learning

Advancing African Language AI

Why low-resource language engineering is the cornerstone of democratizing artificial intelligence in developing economies.

01

Dialectal Nuance & Orthography

Standard multilingual LLMs struggle with Hausa phonetic variations, Latin hooked letters (ɓ, ɗ, ƙ), and loanword transliterations. My pipeline implements subword boundary normalizers that preserve semantic intent across regional Nigerian and Nigerien dialects.

normalize_hausa_spans(tokens, text)
02

Token Classification for Veterinary Health

Using fine-tuned checkpoints from Davlan/afro-xlmr-base, my NER models classify health indicators like "kin cin abinci" (refusing feed), "gudawa" (diarrhea), and "zazzabi" (fever) with high precision, linking symptoms directly to veterinary protocol.

BIO Tagging: B-SYMPTOM, I-SYMPTOM, B-SPECIES
03

Multimodal Diagnostics for Farmers

Integrating Gemini Vision with local contextual agronomy to identify crop blights (e.g., Downy Mildew, Cassava Mosaic) from smartphone leaf photographs, delivering clear localized treatment steps in simple actionable language.

Structured JSON Output: { disease, risk, treatment }
