Background & Philosophy

The intersection of real pastures and deep code.

I am an entrepreneur and technical engineer with a trajectory spanning national payment infrastructure, core banking systems, and agricultural technology.

At Unified Payments Services PLC, I led the technical deployment of EMV 3DS 2.0 solutions across Nigeria's leading merchant gateways—including Remita, Paystack, Flutterwave, and Moniepoint. With Herdbase360, we are replacing informal memory and paper notebooks with species-specific digital workflows for cattle, sheep, goats, and poultry.

Concurrently, my research at the University of Lagos produced the first domain-adapted Hausa Named Entity Recognition system for small-ruminant healthcare, published publicly on Hugging Face. I am currently pursuing an MBA at the Nigerian University of Technology and Management (NUTM) to formalize the bridge between world-class enterprise capital and agricultural transformation.

Vision for Herdbase

Letter from the Founder

"Africa has always had the people, the land, and the livestock to build a great agricultural economy. What we have not always had are the systems to help that economy reach its full potential."

I know this because I have lived it.

Herdbase began with three friends who were also livestock farmers. We knew the early mornings, the rising cost of feed, the uncertainty of the market, and the daily work that goes into raising healthy animals. We also knew what it meant to run a business without the information and systems needed to truly understand it.

Our records lived in notebooks and in memory. We could not always say which animals were profitable, what it really cost to raise them, or where money was being lost. And if someone had asked us to prove the health and performance of the business, we would have struggled to do so.

We knew we were working hard. What we did not have were the tools to make that hard work count for more.

That experience changed our view of the livestock industry. We began to see that these were not simply problems on our own farms. They were problems shared by livestock businesses across the continent. Farmers were working without reliable records. Businesses were making decisions without trusted data. Markets were fragmented. Good livestock operations struggled to prove their value to banks or investors.

We have seen what technology did for finance, commerce, and telecommunications across Africa. Livestock should not be left behind.

We believe every livestock business deserves the chance to become more productive, more organized, and globally competitive—regardless of its size. There is much work ahead, but we know why we started, and we know where we are going.

Mustapha Idris Suleiman Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Herdbase Africa Limited
