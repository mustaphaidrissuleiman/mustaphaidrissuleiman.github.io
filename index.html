I build tools to make livestock farming easier and more profitable.

I’m a livestock farmer, technologist, and entrepreneur. Most of my work revolves around finding practical ways to use technology to solve problems in livestock farming. This includes developing software for farmers, exploring how AI can work with African languages, and helping young people find opportunities in agriculture.

I spend a lot of time working with people across Nigeria, learning, experimenting, and figuring out better ways to get things done.

I want to see more African farms become successful businesses, more young people build their future in agriculture, and more African ideas make their mark on the world.

What I’m building

Herdbase360

The all-in-one app that helps you run a livestock farm like a real business. Farmers use it to count every animal, track what they spend on food and medicine, schedule vaccine reminders, and see their real profits. Farm workers can even send daily updates right through WhatsApp, so nobody has to learn complicated computer programs.

herdbaseafrica.com

Hausa Farm AI

Most AI programs only understand English well. But if you visit a livestock market in Northern Nigeria, everyone speaks Hausa. I labeled over 2,000 real sentences and trained an AI model to understand Hausa farm talk, animal names, and market trades. It's completely free and open for anyone to use on Hugging Face.

Model on Hugging Face

CropScan AI

A phone app that spots crop diseases when you snap a photo. It works completely offline with no Wi-Fi or data connection, because many farms are far away from cell towers.

I live in two worlds that rarely talk to each other: livestock farming and software engineering. Most people think farming is old-fashioned, but in Africa, it’s actually one of the biggest opportunities on earth. The only problem is that millions of farms still run entirely on guesswork.

My mission with Herdbase Africa is simple: give every farmer digital tools so they know their real numbers, protect their herds from disease, and turn livestock into a fun, bankable business.

01 —— THE MISSION
2025
—NOW

Building Herdbase Africa & Smarter Livestock Systems

I founded Herdbase Africa to solve a problem I saw firsthand: farmers buy animals, buy feed, sell months later, and have no idea if they actually made a profit. We built Herdbase360 to track live inventory, feed expenses, veterinary schedules, and honest unit economics per batch.

To make adoption effortless, farm staff log daily animal entries straight through WhatsApp. No complicated software, no training manuals. Just clean data and profitable livestock businesses.

02 —— APPLIED AI
2024
—2025

Teaching AI to Speak African Agriculture

Most AI models today only understand English well. But when farmers trade in Northern Nigeria, everyone speaks Hausa. I spent months annotating thousands of real sentences and fine-tuned language models on Hausa farm talk, livestock names, and market dialogues.

I published the model openly on Hugging Face and built CropScan AI to diagnose crop diseases right from a phone camera—even when completely offline with zero cell service. The AI future must include local farmers.

Securing National Payment Rails at Unified Payments

At Unified Payments Services PLC, I served as Team Lead for Web Merchant Platforms, overseeing the deployment and operational certification of Nigeria's mission-critical EMV 3DS 2.0 transaction authentication servers.

My team secured and routed high-volume payment gateway rails for Nigeria's largest fintech market leaders—including Remita, Paystack, Flutterwave, and Moniepoint—while architecting the Payloft Gateway to eliminate transaction friction and safeguard national payment sovereignty.
