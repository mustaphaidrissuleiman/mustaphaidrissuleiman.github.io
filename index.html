Trajectory & Focus

Engineering leverage across fundamental domains.

I am a software engineer, systems architect, and entrepreneur focused on high-consequence technology infrastructure.

My engineering work spans the full spectrum of modern computing: from architecting national-scale EMV 3DS 2.0 financial switches that process millions in transactions, to fine-tuning transformer language models for indigenous African dialects, to deploying mobile edge computer vision.

I do not believe software should be confined to conventional consumer apps or desktop screens. I apply engineering rigor to critical physical and financial sectors that traditional technology companies overlook. As the founder of Herdbase Africa, I built Nigeria's first systematic operating infrastructure for multi-species livestock production, proving that modern software can organize massive, fragmented real-world economies.

Currently completing an MBA at the Nigerian University of Technology and Management (NUTM) alongside research from the University of Lagos (UNILAG), I bridge deep technical engineering with corporate finance, venture leadership, and institutional strategy.

Core Philosophy

Systems over silos.

"The most valuable problems in emerging markets cannot be solved with superficial software. They require leaders who understand deep technical architecture and ground-level operational reality."

True leverage is found where code meets complex, real-world systems.

Whether engineering payment rails that authenticate transactions across Nigeria's largest banks, or digitizing supply chains that have operated on memory for generations, the fundamental challenge is the same: building resilient systems that withstand uncertainty.

Fragmented markets suffer not from a lack of human effort, but from a deficit of structured information. When businesses operate without reliable records, they cannot measure productivity, access commercial credit, or attract institutional capital.

Technology is the catalyst that converts invisible effort into bankable, verifiable value. We saw how digital rails transformed African telecommunications and financial services over the last two decades. The next frontier is applying that same engineering excellence to industry, agriculture, and intelligent language computing.

I build for permanence, resilience, and measurable economic transformation.
