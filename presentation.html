Research Problem

The Operational vs. Classification Disconnect

🚨 The Core Dilemma in Prior Literature

Academic research predominantly treats fraud as a pure classification problem (optimizing F1-score or accuracy on static datasets).

In operational reality, a high-scoring prediction is useless if:
• It takes too long to infer (> 500ms settlement window).
• It cannot be audited or explained to regulators (NDPA 2023).
• The model suffers concept drift as fraudsters adapt.

🧱 Key Operational Bottlenecks

Extreme Class Imbalance: Legitimate transactions outnumber fraud 10,000 to 1; high false positives hurt legitimate customers.

Institutional Silos: Fraudsters attack multiple banks in sequence; institutions cannot freely share intelligence due to privacy boundaries.

Regulatory Demands: CBN's 2026 AML/CFT Guidelines mandate explainability and automated monitoring.

Slide 3 / 12 Research Gaps & Objectives
Most academic research focuses almost exclusively on maximizing precision, recall, or F1-scores on isolated, static datasets. In production, however, a model that generates an alert without operational escalation paths, without an auditable explanation, or without inter-bank coordination is an incomplete solution. This study addresses that exact architectural void.

Complementary Theoretical Triad

Criminology

Fraud Triangle Theory

Cressey (1953)

Identifies Pressure, Opportunity, and Rationalisation. In digital payments, weak authentication and unmonitored endpoints create the systemic opportunity.

Environmental Criminology

Routine Activity Theory

Cohen & Felson (1979)

Crime occurs when a Motivated Offender meets a Suitable Target in the Absence of Capable Guardianship. Establishes the role of real-time monitoring as automated guardianship.

Organizational Systems

Socio-Technical Theory

Bostrom & Heinen (1977)

A detection model alone cannot prevent fraud. Value depends on the interaction between algorithms, human investigators, policies, and regulatory mandates.

⚡ Analytical Lens: Anomaly Detection & Concept Drift (Lu et al., 2022)

Statistical distribution shifts over time require dynamic pattern learning rather than static decision boundaries.

Here is the core contribution of the study. Notice that this is not a one-way conveyor belt. The layers form a closed-loop socio-technical system. Layer 2 Deep Dive

Multi-Signal Risk Formulation: R = f(T, B, C, A, G)

📐 The Five Component Signals

T (Transaction): Amount, channel, velocity, counterparty type.

B (Behavioral): Historical spending deviations, uncharacteristic hours.

C (Contextual Telemetry): Device ID, IP reputation, cell tower location.

A (Analytical Scoring): Supervised ML probability + unsupervised anomaly scores.

G (Global Intelligence): Cross-bank threat indicators received from Layer 5.

🎯 Calibrated Decision Policy (Table 3.3)

🟢 Low Risk: No suspicious telemetry Approve (<200ms)
🟡 Moderate Risk: Minor uncharacteristic shift Step-up 2FA / Verify
🔴 High Risk: Multiple strong signals Hold & Expedited Review
⛔ Critical Risk: Confirmed compromised credentials Immediate Block
Slide 6 / 12 Algorithm-Agnostic Mathematical Model
The risk relationship R = f(T, B, C, A, G) formally demonstrates that fraud risk is multidimensional. An isolated transaction of 100,000 Naira might look completely normal on its own, but becomes immediately suspicious when combined with a novel device ID, an uncharacteristic 3 AM timestamp, and a destination account newly flagged by another institution.
Operational Architecture (Figure 3.4 / 3.5)

The Dual-Track Architecture: Real-Time vs. Governance

⚡ Track 1: The Synchronous Fast Path

Target Latency: 150 – 300 milliseconds

• Ingests transaction and cached metadata.

• Evaluates composite risk score R.

• Executes decision (Approve, Step-up 2FA, Hold, Block).

• Emits asynchronous audit payload to Track 2.

🔄 Track 2: The Asynchronous Governance & Feedback Path

Execution Budget: Minutes to Days (Offline)

• Stores immutable decision logs & model versions.

• Generates human-readable XAI feature attribution cards.

• Curates confirmed analyst outcomes for retrain validation.

• Exports sanitized IoCs to Layer 5 Institutional Exchange.

Slide 8 / 12 Human-in-the-Loop Governance
In high-stakes financial operations, a black-box probability score of 0.89 is useless to a human investigator or a court of law. Layer 4 generates intuitive feature attribution (inspired by SHAP values and user-centered XAI principles) alongside an immutable audit trail. This satisfies the strict accountability standards of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and CBN's 2026 AML/CFT guidelines.
Layer 5 Deep Dive (Figure 3.3 / 3.7)

Ecosystem-Wide Institutional Coordination

🌐 Institutional Participants

• Commercial & Merchant Banks: Ingest and emit terminal transaction threats.

• Fintechs & Neobanks: Protect high-velocity mobile money endpoints.

• NIBSS: Central instant clearing switch providing network-level correlation.

• NeFF & Regulators: Policy oversight and national crime typology tracking.

🔒 Privacy by Design (NDPA 2023 Compliant)

Strict Prohibition: Zero raw consumer transaction records or PII are shared.

Shared Artifacts:
• Cryptographic hashes of confirmed fraudulent device IDs.
• Account mule network topologies.
• Malicious IP subnets and phishing campaign vectors.

Slide 9 / 12 Multi-Stakeholder Fraud Immunity
A fraudster who successfully exploits a vulnerability at Bank A will frequently move immediately to Fintech B and PSP C. By implementing privacy-preserving threat intelligence exchange, an attack detected at one node can inoculate every participating institution within minutes, without violating consumer data privacy laws.

Scenario C: Midnight Account Takeover

Critical Risk → Instant Intercept & Block

3:00 AM full balance transfer to foreign wallet via new browser ID. Critical threshold triggered, transfer blocked, push alert sent to victim.

Scenario D: Multi-Bank Mule Ring

Emerging Pattern → Layer 5 Ecosystem Inoculation

Syndicate routes stolen funds across 4 fintech wallets. Layer 5 correlates graph topology and disseminates sanitized IoCs across all rails.
